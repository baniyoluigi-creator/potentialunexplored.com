SETUP: SENDING WEBSITE FORMS TO A GOOGLE SHEET
Potential Unexplored Ltd (potentialunexplored.com)

This replaces Formspree. There is no monthly limit. Every application and
enquiry is saved in a Google Sheet, one tab per form, and you also get an
email. It takes about 10 minutes, once.

Until you finish this, the forms keep using Formspree as before, so nothing
breaks while you set it up.


PART 1: Create the sheet and the script

1. Sign in to Google with baniyoluigi@gmail.com and open sheets.new
2. Name the sheet "PUL Applications and Enquiries".
3. Click Extensions, then Apps Script.
4. Delete everything in the editor.
5. Open google-apps-script.gs from this folder, copy ALL of it, and paste
   it into the editor.
6. Click the save icon.


PART 2: Publish it

1. Click Deploy (top right), then New deployment.
2. Click the small gear icon next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
3. Set these three things:
      Description:      PUL website forms
      Execute as:       Me
      Who has access:   Anyone
4. Click Deploy.
5. Authorise access when Google asks. Click Advanced, then
   "Go to (project name) (unsafe)", then Allow. This is normal for your own
   script. It only writes to this sheet and sends you email.
6. Copy the Web app URL. It starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/
   and ends with /exec


PART 3: Connect it to the website

Send the Web app URL to Claude and ask it to switch the PUL forms on.
Or open assets/js/main.js on GitHub (baniyoluigi-creator/potentialunexplored.com),
find this line near the top:
      var SHEET_ENDPOINT = '';
and paste the URL between the quotes, then Commit changes.


PART 4: Test it

1. Wait about a minute for GitHub Pages to update.
2. Send a test message from potentialunexplored.com/contact.html. Wait at
   least 5 seconds before pressing Send.
3. Check the sheet. A tab called "Contact Messages" appears with your test
   entry, and an email arrives in your inbox.
4. Test the MEP application form too. It goes to "MEP Applications".
5. Delete the test rows afterwards.


GOOD TO KNOW

* To change the script later, use Deploy, then Manage deployments, then the
  pencil icon, then Version: New version, then Deploy. The link stays the same.
* To also send notices to info@potentialunexplored.com, put the addresses in
  NOTIFY_EMAIL at the top of the script, separated by commas.
* Free Gmail can send about 100 notification emails a day. Past that, entries
  are still saved in the sheet and only the email is skipped.
* New form fields appear in the sheet as new columns automatically.
* Share the sheet with your team so they can follow up.
