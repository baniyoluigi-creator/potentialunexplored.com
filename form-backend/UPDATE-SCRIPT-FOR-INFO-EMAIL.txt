UPDATE: GET PUL FORM ALERTS ON info@potentialunexplored.com
Do this once. The web app link stays the same, so the website does not change.

1. Sign in to Google with baniyoluigi@gmail.com and open the sheet "Boys to Men Registrations".
2. Click Extensions, then Apps Script.
3. Delete everything in the editor.
4. Open google-apps-script-with-pul-email.gs from this folder, copy ALL of it, and paste it into the editor.
5. Click the save icon.
6. Click Deploy, then Manage deployments, then the pencil icon.
7. Under Version choose "New version", then click Deploy.
   Please do not click "New deployment". That would create a new link.
8. Google may ask you to authorise again. Click Authorise access, choose your account,
   then Advanced, then "Go to (project name) (unsafe)", then Allow.

After this, every form on potentialunexplored.com sends an email to info@potentialunexplored.com
and to baniyoluigi@gmail.com. Boys to Men forms keep going to the owner only, as before.
MEP Masterclass applications now go to their own tab called "MEP Applications" in the same sheet,
separate from the camp "Registrations" tab. Other entries are still saved in the sheet, with PUL ones marked "PUL website" in the Form column.

Test: send a message from potentialunexplored.com/contact.html (wait 5 seconds before pressing Send)
and check that it arrives at info@potentialunexplored.com.

To use a different or extra address later, change PUL_NOTIFY_EMAIL near the top of the script.
Free Gmail can send about 100 notification emails a day. Past that, entries are still saved in the sheet.
