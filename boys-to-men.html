Boys to Men: Life Readiness Programme

7-Day Residential Camp  |  8 Camps Delivered  |  10 School Outreach Programmes  |  Over 30,000 Young People Reached

UGX 400,000 / USD 120 Arua intake  |  UGX 500,000 / USD 150 Kampala

Age Clusters

Junior: Ages 10 to 13 Teen: Ages 14 to 17 Young Adult: Ages 18 to 23

What changes in a young man who goes through this

  • He discovers who he is, beyond the labels and pressures that have shaped him
  • He confronts what has been holding him back: past hurts, unhealthy habits, destructive patterns
  • He gains direction for his future by identifying his strengths and developing a practical life plan
  • He develops financial intelligence: money management, value creation, and entrepreneurship
  • He becomes a more confident leader and communicator
  • He develops a healthier understanding of manhood, including collaborating with empowered women
  • He joins a brotherhood of peers who share his values and support his growth long after the camp ends

What the sessions explore

Mindset and identity Healing from trauma Overcoming addiction Understanding sexuality and manhood Emotional intelligence Confidence and self-worth Purpose and life direction Career guidance Financial literacy Entrepreneurship Public speaking and leadership Pan-African identity Positive masculinity Applied entrepreneurship projects

🏠
Day 7: The Family Coronation

On the final morning, from 8 am to 1 pm, parents and guardians join a dedicated half-day session on parenting, covering what their son has been learning and equipping the home with the same tools. The camp closes with an official coronation ceremony, celebrating each young man's commitment to the man he has chosen to become. The transformation does not end at the gate.

Safeguarding

Every camp is staffed by vetted facilitators and coaches, with a minimum adult-to-participant ratio maintained throughout, basic first aid available on site, and a clear point of contact for parents at all times during the camp. Full safeguarding details are shared with every family at the point of registration.
