Support Our Work

Sponsorships, in-kind support, and partnerships that extend Organisational Strengthening and Life Readiness work further across Africa.

Every camp intake, school outreach visit, and coaching engagement we deliver is made possible by people and institutions who choose to stand behind this work, sometimes with funding, sometimes with materials, transport, venues, or expertise, and sometimes simply by opening a door to a school, company, or community that needs what we offer. If you have been shaped by one of our programmes, or you simply believe in what we are building, there is a place for you here.

Corporate Sponsorship

Sponsor a Boys to Men or Girls Rising intake, a school outreach visit, or a cohort of the MEP Masterclass for participants who could not otherwise afford it. Sponsorship packages include visibility for your organisation across the relevant programme materials.

In-Kind Support

Venues, transport, meals, camp equipment, printed materials, or professional services such as design, photography, or videography all directly reduce the cost of delivering a programme.

Facilitation Partnerships

If you are a trained facilitator, coach, or counsellor who wants to volunteer time to a camp or school outreach visit, we would like to hear from you.

Individual Giving

One-time or recurring individual contributions toward camp scholarships for young people who cannot afford the full participation fee.
