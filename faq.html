Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Common questions about registration, payment, and what to expect across our programmes.

General

How do I register for a programme?

Go to the Register page, choose the programme or intake you want, and fill in the form. Every registration is confirmed by our team within 24 hours.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer, MTN Mobile Money, and Airtel Money. Payment details and instructions are shown once you select a programme on the Register page.

Will I receive confirmation after I register or pay?

Yes. Every form on this site sends us an email immediately, and our team follows up directly, usually within 24 hours, by phone, email, or WhatsApp, whichever you provide.

Can institutions request a customised proposal instead of a standard package?

Yes. Every institutional Organisational Strengthening engagement, whether Leadership Development, Team Building, or Sales Excellence, is quoted individually based on group size, duration, and scope. Use the Institutional Enquiry form on the Register page or the Contact page to start that conversation.

I registered but need to change or cancel. What should I do?

Contact us directly on WhatsApp at +256 775 495 431 or email info@potentialunexplored.com as soon as possible, and our team will talk you through the options for your specific programme and payment stage.

Boys to Men and Girls Rising

What age groups does Boys to Men accept?

Boys to Men runs three age clusters within the same camp: Junior (10-13), Teen (14-17), and Young Adult (18-23), each with age-appropriate sessions built on the same core Life Readiness curriculum.

How long is the Boys to Men camp?

It is a 7-day residential camp. Day 7 is a half-day, running from 8am to 1pm, and includes a parenting session for guardians followed by the closing coronation ceremony.

Are there female coaches at Girls Rising?

Yes, Girls Rising is delivered with female coaches throughout the programme.

What should my child bring to camp?

A full packing list and preparation checklist is shared directly with registered families ahead of each intake. If you have registered and have not received it, contact us on WhatsApp.

Is safeguarding taken seriously at your camps?

Yes. Please see our Safeguarding and Child Protection Policy page for the full detail on how we protect every young person in our care.

Can't find your question here? Contact us directly and we will respond within 24 hours.
