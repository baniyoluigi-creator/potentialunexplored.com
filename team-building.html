Team Building Events

A well-designed team experience surfaces what a year of meetings cannot: the real state of trust, communication, and cohesion in a team. Our events are energising, participatory, and genuinely enjoyable. Beneath the surface, every activity is diagnostic and transformative work, designed to surface a specific area of team performance and create the conditions for the team to address it together. Teams do not leave with a certificate. They leave changed.

  • Communication: whether information flows clearly and real conversations happen in the room
  • Conflict: whether disagreement is handled productively or suppressed until it becomes damaging
  • Cohesion: whether people genuinely trust one another and want each other to succeed
  • Leadership within the team: whether people take initiative and step up when it matters
  • Accountability and commitment: whether what is agreed in meetings is actually done
  • Work-life balance and team wellbeing: whether the pace and culture are sustainable
  • Reporting and feedback culture: whether people feel safe to surface problems early
  • Shared purpose and values: whether everyone understands what they are collectively working toward

Past engagements include Centenary Bank across 12 branches, African Women Rising, CORDAID South Sudan, and Opportunity International with participants from approximately 10 countries. That 2025 OI engagement became the foundation of a year-long coaching programme for over 30 managers, currently ongoing.

Team building in Uganda

Team building that gets to the real issues

Potential Unexplored Ltd facilitates team building events for organisations across Uganda and Africa. Our events are full of games, laughter and movement, but every activity is chosen for a reason. Each one surfaces something real about how your team communicates, trusts, disagrees and delivers, and each is followed by an honest, guided conversation so the team leaves with clear agreements, not just good memories.

In short. We design and facilitate one day, half day and multi day team building events for NGOs, banks, universities, companies and faith based organisations anywhere in Uganda and Africa. Of our more than 130 engagements in the last five years, 56 have been in team building, leadership and management.

Sample agendas

Every event is designed around your team, so these are examples rather than fixed packages. They show how a typical day flows.

Our most requested format, roughly 8:00am to 5:00pm

Our approach

What makes our team building different

  • Every game has a purpose. Activities are chosen to surface a specific issue, such as trust, communication, accountability or conflict, not just to fill time.
  • The debrief is where the change happens. After each activity we guide the team to name what happened and connect it to real work, in a way that feels safe rather than exposing.
  • Proven frameworks, explained simply. We draw on our own CIVEC Trust Framework (Competence, Integrity, Vulnerability, Empathy, Consistency), our Team Accountability Compass and well known models such as Patrick Lencioni's Five Dysfunctions of a Team.
  • Participatory and fun. Games, role plays, stories, group work and very little PowerPoint, so people stay engaged the whole day.
  • Clear outcomes. Teams leave with agreed actions and owners, and you receive a short written report with our observations and recommendations.

How we work with you

From first conversation to follow up

Every engagement begins with a conversation. We listen to what is happening with your team, what has already been tried, and what you want to see change. Only then do we shape the programme, so that it fits your people and is not a copy of what we did for someone else.

  1. Listen. A short call or visit to understand your people, your setting and your goals.
  2. Propose. A written proposal with the content, the days, the venue needs and the cost, in shillings.
  3. Deliver. Participatory sessions led by our facilitators, with real stories, exercises and time to practise.
  4. Follow up. A short report and a check in after the sessions, so the learning is not lost when everyone returns to work.
