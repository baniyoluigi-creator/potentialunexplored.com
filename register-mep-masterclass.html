Pay to secure your place

Payment is by bank transfer or deposit into our Centenary Bank account. You can pay from your bank, or send from MTN MoMo or Airtel Money to the bank account. Not sure how? Choose the way that suits you below and follow the simple steps. It takes only a few minutes.

Payment for your placeThe MEP Masterclass fee is UGX 500,000 or USD 150. A booking fee of UGX 50,000 (USD 15) secures your place. It is non-refundable, but it counts toward the total fee. At least half of the fee (UGX 250,000 or USD 75) must be paid before the programme starts, and the balance within one month.

  1. Open your bank app or visit your bank, or any Centenary Bank branch or agent.
  2. Choose to send money to another bank account, or to make a deposit.

  1. On your MTN line, dial *165#
  2. Choose Financial Services.
  3. Choose Bank Transfer and select Centenary Bank.
  4. Enter the account number shown above, then enter the amount.
  5. Enter your MoMo PIN to confirm.
  6. Wait for the MTN confirmation message and keep it. You will need it below.
  1. On your Airtel line, dial *185#
  2. Choose Financial Services, then Banks.
  3. Select Centenary Bank. If you do not see it, choose Next until it appears.
  4. Enter the account number shown above, then enter the amount.
  5. Enter your Airtel Money PIN to confirm.
  6. Wait for the Airtel confirmation message and keep it. You will need it below.
Good to know. The menu on your phone may look a little different, so follow what appears on your screen. Mobile money has daily limits and small charges. Check your limit before you start. If the amount is more than your limit, pay in parts on different days and send proof for each part. Money is always sent to the Centenary Bank account above, never to a phone number.
